Motion and freshness

Score changesRow and card flash lime 30% to clear, 1.2s ease-out, once. Live region announces "Goal, Chelsea 2 Man Utd 1".
Live minute6px lime dot pulses 1.6s. The minute itself never animates.
New commentArrives under a "3 new" chip; the list never jumps while you read. Tap the chip to scroll.
Match sheetSlides up 240ms ease-out, swipe down to close; desktop panel slides in from the right 200ms.
Reduced motionNo flash, no pulse, no slide. States change instantly; the live region still speaks.
Updated 8s ago Delayed · score is 2 min old Showing last known score · 14 min

The age shown is the age of the data at the source, never the time since the browser fetched. Over 2 minutes live is "delayed"; the state names the age rather than hiding it.
